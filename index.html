    About

    My path runs from product engineering at PeopleStrong to PwC, where I brought order to PepsiCo’s supply chain KPIs and picked up a promotion, and on to a data science master’s at Maryland that put my models into daily use.

    I love getting my hands dirty in data. I dig in until I understand it, sketch it until it tells a story, then build the pipelines and AI agents that keep it working long after I’ve moved on. Messy datasets don’t scare me. I find them outlier-ly fascinating, and nothing beats the moment a stubborn table finally agrees to join the conversation.

    Off the clock, I write about data on Medium and contribute to open source, including Debezium.
